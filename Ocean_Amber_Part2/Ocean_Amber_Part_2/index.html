The Ocean Amber feeling

Unhurried dining, beautifully considered.

Ocean Amber brings coastal freshness and South African warmth to a modern dining room. Our plates are designed around contrast: bright citrus, smoky fire, delicate seafood and the deep sweetness of amber-toned spices.
