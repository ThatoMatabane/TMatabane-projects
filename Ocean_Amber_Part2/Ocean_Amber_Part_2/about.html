Our story

A restaurant shaped
by light, tide & fire.

Ocean Amber is a fictional academic restaurant concept built around a simple idea: memorable dining comes from thoughtful details.

Our philosophy

Coastal soul.
Modern table.

Ocean Amber was imagined as a meeting point between the freshness of the coast and the warmth of South African hospitality.

Our visual identity follows the same contrast. Deep ocean navy creates calm and sophistication, while amber gold introduces warmth and energy. The result is a digital experience that mirrors the restaurant itself: refined, welcoming and memorable.

From the typography to the responsive grid, each design choice is intentional. The site uses an external stylesheet, reusable classes, Flexbox, CSS Grid, relative units and responsive breakpoints to deliver a consistent experience across desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

What guides us

Three simple values

01

Seasonal

Menus respond to what is fresh, allowing ingredients to lead the experience rather than follow a fixed formula.

02

Warm

Hospitality should feel personal. Every interaction is designed to be relaxed, attentive and genuine.

03

Considered

Good design is quiet but purposeful — from the plate presentation to the spacing and hierarchy of this website.
