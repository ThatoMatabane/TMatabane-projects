PASTIMES

Project Presentation & Technical Report

SOUTH AFRICA 2024 PHP & MYSQL ARCHITECTURE

1. Project Overview

Pastimes is a sophisticated, responsive e-commerce platform designed to modernize the second-hand fashion industry in South Africa. Unlike generic marketplaces, Pastimes focuses on sustainability and high-end curation, localized with South African Rands (ZAR) and specialized categories such as the "Sneaker Vault."

Login & Registration

Security is handled at the core. The registration system uses password_hash (Bcrypt) to ensure data integrity. Upon login, a secure session is initiated, granting users access to customized dashboards based on their roles (Buyer/Seller).

Dynamic Catalog

The shop features an advanced PHP query engine. Users can filter items by categories like Womenswear, Menswear, and Sneakers. Prices are dynamically formatted to ZAR, reflecting affordable thrift-market rates.

3. Interactive Experience

To bridge the gap between static PHP and modern apps, we integrated JavaScript-based Toast Notifications. Users receive instant visual cues when items are added to their cart. Additionally, the Sustainability Tracker highlights the positive environmental impact (Water savings) for every piece of thrifted clothing.
